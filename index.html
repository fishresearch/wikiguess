How to play

Name the Wikipedia article in as few clues as you can.

1
Each clue is a real section of the article, with its name blacked out. Clues start obscure and get easier.
Its blood is blue because of the protein hemocyanin, and a ███████ has three hearts.
2
Type your guess. Any Wikipedia article counts, and typos are OK. A wrong guess or a skip reveals the next clue. You get 6 clues.
3
Link Radar: wrong guesses show how close you are, plus pages your guess and the answer both link to.

4
Stuck? Tap 💡 Hint to see what kind of thing it is, then the shape of the title. Hints are free, but they show up as 💡 when you share.
5
New daily puzzles in every category, or play Endless. Share your result grid: 🟦🟨🟧🟩

    How to play: each clue is a real section of a Wikipedia article, with the subject's name blacked out. Clues start obscure and end with the introduction. You have six clues; every wrong guess or skip reveals the next one.

    Link Radar: every wrong guess also shows how close it is to the answer on Wikipedia's web of links, along with a few pages the two articles both link to. A good wrong guess can be the best clue you get.
